Shipping & Return Guidelines

1. Domestic Pan-India Shipping

At AALAYA, we take immense pride in ensuring your luxury creations travel safely from our looms to your doorstep. All domestic orders exceeding ₹50,000 receive complimentary insured priority air express delivery. Standard shipping for orders under ₹50,000 is billed at a flat fee of ₹350.

Dispatch Timelines:
• Ready-to-Ship Pret & In-Stock Sarees: Dispatched within 24 to 48 hours.
• Made-to-Order Kurtis & Tailored Sets: 7 to 10 business days.
• Hand-Embroidered Bridal Lehengas: 4 to 6 weeks.

2. White-Glove Hand Courier (Delhi-NCR & Mumbai)

For patrons located within the National Capital Region (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida) and Mumbai Metropolitan Region, we offer dedicated white-glove hand delivery. An Aalaya concierge courier will deliver your garments on padded satin hangers inside our signature breathable travel covers within 24 to 48 hours.

3. 7-Day Hassle-Free Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be completely delighted with your heirloom piece. If for any reason your selection does not meet your expectations, we gladly accept returns and exchanges within 7 calendar days of receipt.

Conditions for Return:
• Garments must remain unworn, unwashed, and free of fragrance, cosmetics, or soil.
• All security tags, Silk Mark tags, and designer labels must remain intact.
• Garments must be returned in the original gold-embossed keepsake chest and protective muslin cloth.
• Custom-stitched blouses and bespoke bridal lehengas modified to personal measurements cannot be refunded; however, complimentary adjustments and alterations will be performed by our atelier until you are 100% satisfied.

4. International Global Shipping

We ship to over 65 countries worldwide via DHL Express Priority. International transit times typically range from 4 to 7 business days depending on destination customs clearance. Applicable import duties and local taxes are calculated transparently during international checkout.
