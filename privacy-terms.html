All transactions are denominated in Indian Rupees (INR) inclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST). International orders may be settled in USD, GBP, or EUR according to real-time bank conversion rates. 4. Artisanal Nuances & Handloom Variances

Because each AALAYA creation is spun, woven, and embroidered by hand on artisanal wooden looms, subtle variations in texture, weave slubs, and color shading are inherent hallmarks of authentic craftsmanship. These are not flaws, but rather the unique signature of the human hand that shaped your garment.
