OUR GENESIS

Custodians of Sacred Craft.

Founded to bridge the timeless majesty of Indian textile heritage with the precision of contemporary international couture.

THE PHILOSOPHY

Where Generational Looms Meet Modern Silhouettes

AALAYA was conceived as an altar to Indian artisanal genius. In a world inundated with synthetic fast fashion and machine replicas, we stand resolutely as custodians of slow luxury. Every silk filament, every beaten zari ribbon, and every needle stitch honors a lineage of craft passed down like sacred whispers from mother to daughter, father to son.

OUR WEAVING GUILDS

Four Historic Clusters

UTTAR PRADESH

Varanasi: Katan Silk & Kadhwa Zari

Over 120 master weavers working on traditional pit looms in Bunkar Tola, painstakingly interlacing pure mulberry silk warps with gold-plated zari bootas.

AWADH

Lucknow: The 32 Stitches of Chikankari

Empowering over 300 women craftswomen across rural Lucknow who preserve the delicate shadow stitch (bakhiya) and metallic mukaish needle dots.

TAMIL NADU

Kanchipuram: Korvai Temple Weft

Heavy, sculptural double-warp silks interlocking contrast borders with ancient temple gopuram motifs, certified Silk Mark India.

NEW DELHI & MUMBAI

The Atelier: Haute Couture & Pret

Our master patternmakers sculpt artisanal textiles into international evening gowns, kimono wrap co-ords, and structured corsets.
